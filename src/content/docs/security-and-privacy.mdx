A short, practical summary of how FormWire treats data. The authoritative documents are the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Terms of Service](/terms).

## Two roles: controller and processor

FormWire handles two kinds of personal data, with different responsibilities:

- **Your account data** (your email, name, billing). Here FormWire is the **controller**.
- **Submissions to your forms** (data from your visitors). Here **you** are the controller and FormWire is the **processor**, handling that data on your instructions.

As the controller of your form data, you're responsible for having a lawful basis to collect it, telling your visitors about it (e.g. a privacy notice on your site), and handling their data-subject requests. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) covering FormWire's processor role is available on request.

## What we capture with submissions

Alongside your form fields, FormWire records technical metadata for each submission: IP address, user agent, referrer, and request origin, plus derived geo and device information and any UTM parameters. This supports spam filtering, validation, and analytics. See [How It Works](/getting-started/how-it-works).

## What we don't do

- We don't sell personal data.
- We don't use your form submissions to train AI models.
- We don't read your submissions except where necessary to operate the service (e.g. automated spam analysis or debugging an issue you raise).

## Security measures

- Encryption in transit (TLS) for all traffic.
- Passwords stored only as salted hashes.
- Access controls on production data.
- Spam and threat detection on inbound submissions.

To report a security issue, email **security@formwire.app**.

## Your data, your control

- **Rotate** a form secret if it's exposed — see [Managing Forms](/guides/managing-forms).
- **Get your data out** — see [Exporting Data](/guides/exporting-data).
- **Delete** submissions, forms, or your whole account from the dashboard.
