## How integrations work

1. You create an integration under **Integrations** in your account settings.
2. You link it to one or more forms in each form's **Integrations** tab.
3. When a linked form stores a submission with the `new` status, FormWire sends it to each active integration.

Things to know:

- Submissions flagged as `spam` or `pending_review` are not sent. If you change one back to `new`, you can send it with **Retry failed**.
- You can turn an integration off without deleting it.
- If a delivery fails, FormWire emails you and records the failure on the submission. FormWire doesn't retry automatically. You retry from the submission. See [Submissions](/guides/submissions#retry-a-failed-integration).

## Where your data goes

An integration sends submission data to a destination that FormWire doesn't control and that has its own terms. You are responsible for the destinations you choose. See the [Terms of Service](https://formwire.app/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://formwire.app/privacy).
