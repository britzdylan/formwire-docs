FormWire has three plans. They differ in how many submissions you can receive each month, how many people you can forward notifications to, and the support you get. Every plan includes unlimited forms.

## Plans

| Plan | Submissions per month | Forms | Notification recipients per form | Support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | 50 | Unlimited | None | Community |
| **Pro** | 5,000 | Unlimited | 3 | Email |
| **Business** | 50,000 | Unlimited | 10 | Priority |

Starter is free. See the [pricing page](https://formwire.app/pricing) for current prices.

## How the submission quota works

Your quota counts the submissions stored across all of your forms in the current period. Submissions flagged as spam or pending review count, because they are stored.

- On **Pro** and **Business**, the period is your billing period.
- On **Starter**, the period is the calendar month.

Your usage for the current period is shown in your billing settings.

## What happens at the limit

There are two limits, and they work independently:

- **Your plan's quota.** When your account reaches it, all of your forms reject new submissions until the period resets or you upgrade.
- **A form's own limit.** You can set a limit on a single form. When the form reaches it, that form rejects new submissions until its window resets. See [Managing Forms](/guides/managing-forms#submission-limits).

A rejected submission is not stored and can't be recovered, and it doesn't count toward your usage. The sender gets a `429` response. The [React guide](/frameworks/react#handle-the-response) lists the error codes.
