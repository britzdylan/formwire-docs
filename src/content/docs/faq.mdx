## Why can't I see my submission?

Check these in order:

1. **The status.** The submission may have been stored as `pending_review` or `spam`. Search the form's submissions for those statuses. See [Submissions](/guides/submissions).
2. **The form.** A disabled form rejects every submission.
3. **Your limits.** A form that has reached its own limit, or an account that has reached its plan's quota, rejects new submissions. See [Plans & Limits](/billing/plans-and-limits).
4. **The endpoint.** Your form must post to `https://api.formwire.app/v1/submit/YOUR_FORM_SECRET` with the current secret. If you regenerated the secret, the old endpoint no longer works.

If the request reached FormWire and was rejected, the response contains an error code that tells you why. The [React guide](/frameworks/react#handle-the-response) lists the codes.

## Do I need an API key to submit?

No. The form secret in the URL identifies your form. Submitting doesn't need a token or an API key.

## Is it safe to put the endpoint in client-side code?

Yes. The endpoint is meant to be called from the browser. Anyone who views your page can see the secret, and the worst they can do with it is submit to your form. If that happens, [regenerate the secret](/guides/managing-forms#regenerate-the-form-secret).

## Why was a real message marked as spam?

It triggered a spam signal. Either the honeypot field had a value, the form was submitted within 2 seconds of loading, or a field contained text that looks like an attack. The submission's security details show which one. You can change its status, and [Spam Protection](/guides/spam-protection) explains the scoring.

## How large can a submission be?

A submission can be at most **50&nbsp;KB** and must contain at least one field.

## Is there a rate limit?

Yes. Each IP address can send up to 30 requests per minute to a form. Requests over that limit get a `429` response.

## Can I upload files?

Not yet. Submissions can't include file uploads.

## How do I send submissions to another tool?

Create a [webhook integration](/integrations/webhooks). It sends each new submission as JSON to a URL you choose. Slack, GitHub, and Zapier integrations are [planned](/integrations).

## How do I get my data out?

Export a form's submissions as a CSV file from its **Submissions** tab. See [Exporting Data](/guides/exporting-data).

## How do I get help?

Email **support@formwire.app**. Pro and Business plans include email support, and Business requests are handled first. See [Plans & Limits](/billing/plans-and-limits).
