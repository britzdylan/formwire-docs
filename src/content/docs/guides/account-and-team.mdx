Manage your FormWire account from your settings.

## Profile

Set your **name** and upload a **profile picture (avatar)**. Your name is used for personalization in the dashboard.

## Sign-in methods

You can sign in with:

- **Email and password** — your password is stored only as a salted hash.

## Security

- **Change your password** from the security section of your settings.
- If you believe your account has been compromised, contact **security@formwire.app** right away.

## Notification preferences

Control which emails FormWire sends you — security alerts, product updates, the weekly digest, and marketing. See [Email Notifications](/guides/email-notifications) for what each covers.

## Deleting your account

:::danger
You can delete your account from your settings. Deletion removes your account and associated data, subject to the retention rules in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy). [Export](/guides/exporting-data) anything you want to keep first — deletion can't be undone.
:::
