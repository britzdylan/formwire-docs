Manage your subscription from your billing settings. Payments are processed by **Creem**.

## Subscription status

Your subscription can be in one of several states, shown in your billing settings:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `trialing` | In a free trial. |
| `active` | Paid and current. |
| `scheduled_cancel` | Will cancel at the end of the current period. |
| `canceled` | Ended. |
| `paused` | Temporarily suspended. |
| `unpaid` | A payment is outstanding. |

## Updating payment details

Update your payment method through the billing portal linked from your settings. Creem handles your card details securely — FormWire never stores your card number.

## Canceling

Cancel from your billing settings. Cancellation takes effect at the **end of your current billing period**, so you keep access until then. After it ends, your account moves to the free Starter plan and its limits apply.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the subscription is marked `unpaid` and we'll attempt to recover it. If it isn't resolved, access to paid limits is suspended until payment succeeds or you switch plans.

:::caution
Submissions received while over the free limit during this time may be rejected — see [Plans & Limits](/billing/plans-and-limits).
:::
