This page summarizes how FormWire handles data. The [Privacy Policy](https://formwire.app/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://formwire.app/terms) are the binding documents.

## Who is responsible for which data

FormWire handles two kinds of personal data, and the responsibility differs for each:

- **Your account data** is your email address, name, and billing details. FormWire is the **controller** of this data.
- **Your form submissions** are the data your visitors send. You are the **controller** of this data, and FormWire is the **processor** that handles it on your instructions.

As the controller of your submissions, you need a lawful basis for collecting them. You also need to tell your visitors what you collect, for example in a privacy notice on your site, and respond to their requests about their data.

A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) that covers FormWire's role as processor is available on request.

## What FormWire stores with each submission

FormWire stores your form fields and these details about the request:

- IP address, user agent, referrer, and request origin
- Approximate location and device information, derived from the IP address and user agent
- UTM parameters from the referrer

FormWire shows these details to you on each submission. Mention them in your own privacy notice. [How It Works](/getting-started/how-it-works#captured-metadata) has the full list.

## What FormWire doesn't do

- FormWire doesn't sell personal data.
- FormWire doesn't use your submissions to train AI models.
- FormWire staff don't read your submissions, except when that is needed to run the service, such as debugging a problem you report. Automated spam analysis does process every submission.

## Security measures

- All traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS.
- Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
- Access to production data is restricted.
- Every incoming submission is checked for spam and attack patterns.

To report a security issue, email **security@formwire.app**.

## Control your data

- **Regenerate** a form secret if it is being abused. See [Managing Forms](/guides/managing-forms#regenerate-the-form-secret).
- **Export** your submissions. See [Exporting Data](/guides/exporting-data).
- **Delete** submissions, forms, or your whole account in the dashboard.
