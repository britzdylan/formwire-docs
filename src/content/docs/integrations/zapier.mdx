## Connect Zapier with a webhook

Zapier's **Webhooks by Zapier** app has a **Catch Hook** trigger that accepts FormWire's webhook requests.

<Steps>

1. In Zapier, create a Zap with the **Catch Hook** trigger and copy the webhook URL Zapier gives you.
2. In FormWire, create a [webhook integration](/integrations/webhooks) with that URL and link it to your forms.
3. Submit your form once so that Zapier receives a sample request.
4. In your Zap's actions, use the fields from the `data` object.

</Steps>
