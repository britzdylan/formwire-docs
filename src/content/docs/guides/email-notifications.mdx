FormWire can email you each time a form receives a submission. You turn this on for each form. Your account settings control the other emails FormWire sends you.

## Form notifications

Each form has an **Email notifications** toggle in its **Notifications** tab. When it is on, FormWire does two things for each submission the form stores:

- It sends an email to your account's email address.
- It adds a notification to your dashboard inbox.

You are notified about every stored submission, including the ones flagged as spam or pending review. If a form receives a lot of submissions, you may prefer to turn notifications off and use the dashboard or an [integration](/integrations).

## Forward notifications to other recipients

On the Pro and Business plans, you can add other email addresses to a form's notifications. Each recipient is copied on the notification email.

| Plan | Additional recipients per form |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | None |
| Pro | 3 |
| Business | 10 |

Recipients only receive emails while the form's notifications are on.

If you downgrade and a form has more recipients than your new plan allows, FormWire stops forwarding for that form and emails you about it. You still receive the notification yourself. Forwarding starts again when you remove recipients or upgrade.

## Integration failure notifications

If an [integration](/integrations) fails to deliver a submission, FormWire emails you and adds a notification to your dashboard inbox. You can retry the delivery from the submission. See [Submissions](/guides/submissions#retry-a-failed-integration).

## Account email preferences

You choose which other emails you receive under **Notifications** in your account settings:

| Preference | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Security alerts** | Security notifications about your account. You can't turn these off. |
| **Product updates** | Product changes and improvements. |
| **Weekly digest** | A weekly summary of your activity. |
| **Marketing** | New features, promotions, and offers. |
