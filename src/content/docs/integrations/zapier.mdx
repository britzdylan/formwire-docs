## Use a webhook today

Zapier's **Webhooks by Zapier → Catch Hook** trigger works with FormWire right now:

<Steps>

1. In Zapier, create a Zap with the **Catch Hook** trigger and copy the custom webhook URL it gives you.
2. In FormWire, create a [webhook integration](/integrations/webhooks) pointing at that URL and attach it to your forms.
3. Map the submission fields in the `data` object to the actions in your Zap.

</Steps>

This gives you access to Zapier's full catalog of apps before the native integration lands.
