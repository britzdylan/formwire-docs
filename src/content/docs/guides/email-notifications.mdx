FormWire can email you when submissions come in, and keeps you informed about your account. Notifications are controlled at two levels: per form, and per account.

## Per-form notifications

Each form has a **Notifications enabled** toggle. When it's on, FormWire emails you about new submissions to that form. Turn it off for high-volume forms where you'd rather rely on the dashboard or an [integration](/integrations) instead.

You can also see submission activity as **in-app notifications** in your dashboard inbox, independent of email.

## Account email preferences

Your account controls which categories of email FormWire sends you. Manage these in your account settings:

| Preference | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Security alerts** | Important account and security events. Required to keep on. |
| **Product updates** | New features and meaningful changes. |
| **Weekly digest** | A periodic summary of your form activity. |
| **Marketing** | Occasional product news and tips. Opt-in. |

Each can be toggled independently. Security alerts exist to keep your account safe and are kept separate from marketing for that reason.

## Failed delivery

If a notification can't be delivered, the failure is recorded against the submission's processing details. The submission itself is still stored.
