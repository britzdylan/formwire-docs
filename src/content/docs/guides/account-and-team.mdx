You manage your FormWire account in your account settings.

## Profile

You can change your **name** and upload a **profile picture**.

You can't change your email address yourself yet. FormWire sends form notifications to this address, so email **support@formwire.app** if you need it changed.

## Sign-in

You sign in with your email address and password. FormWire stores your password only as a salted hash.

## Password

Change your password under **Security** in your account settings. If you forget it, use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page to get a reset link by email.

If you think someone else has accessed your account, email **security@formwire.app**.

## Email preferences

Under **Notifications**, you choose which emails FormWire sends you. See [Email Notifications](/guides/email-notifications#account-email-preferences).

## Delete your account

:::danger
Deleting your account deletes your forms, your submissions, and your other data, and you can't undo it. [Export](/guides/exporting-data) what you want to keep first. Some records may be kept for the periods described in the [Privacy Policy](https://formwire.app/privacy).
:::
