You manage your subscription under **Billing** in your account settings. That page shows your plan, your usage for the current period, and your recent invoices.

Payments are handled by **Creem**. FormWire never sees or stores your card number.

## Change your plan or payment method

Click **Manage subscription** in your billing settings. This opens the Creem billing portal, where you can change your plan and update your payment method.

## Cancel

Cancel from your billing settings. Your paid plan stays active until the end of the billing period you have already paid for. After that, your account moves to the free Starter plan and its limits apply.

## Subscription status

Your billing settings show one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning | Plan limits that apply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `trialing` | You are in a trial. | Your paid plan |
| `active` | Your subscription is paid and current. | Your paid plan |
| `scheduled_cancel` | You canceled, and the subscription ends when the current period ends. | Your paid plan |
| `canceled` | The subscription has ended. | Starter |
| `paused` | The subscription is paused. | Starter |
| `unpaid` | A payment failed and is still outstanding. | Starter |

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, your subscription becomes `unpaid` and the Starter limits apply until the payment goes through. Update your payment method in the billing portal to fix it.

:::caution
While the Starter limits apply, your forms reject submissions once your account passes 50 for the period. Rejected submissions are not stored. See [Plans & Limits](/billing/plans-and-limits).
:::
