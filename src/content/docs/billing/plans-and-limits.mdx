FormWire plans differ mainly by how many submissions you can receive per billing period. All plans include **unlimited forms**.

## Plans

| Plan | Submissions / period | Forms | Support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | 50 | Unlimited | Community |
| **Pro** | 5,000 | Unlimited | Email |
| **Business** | 50,000 | Unlimited | Priority |

See the [pricing page](/pricing) for current prices.

## How the submission quota works

Your plan's submission limit is counted across **all your forms** within the current billing period. When you reach it, new submissions are rejected until the period resets or you upgrade.

This is separate from a **per-form submission limit**, which you can set yourself to cap an individual form on a daily/weekly/monthly/yearly basis — see [Managing Forms](/guides/managing-forms).

## What happens at the limit

- **Per-form limit reached** → that form rejects submissions until its window resets.
- **Plan quota reached** → all forms reject new submissions until the billing period resets or you upgrade.

Rejected submissions are not stored and don't count toward usage.
