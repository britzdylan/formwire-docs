A standard HTML form can submit to FormWire directly. You don't need JavaScript.

## Basic form

Set the form's `action` to your FormWire endpoint and its `method` to `POST`:

```html
<form action="https://api.formwire.app/v1/submit/YOUR_FORM_SECRET" method="POST">
  <label for="name">Name</label>
  <input type="text" id="name" name="name" required />

  <label for="email">Email</label>
  <input type="email" id="email" name="email" required />

  <label for="message">Message</label>
  <textarea id="message" name="message"></textarea>

  <button type="submit">Send</button>
</form>
```

Each `name` attribute becomes a field in the stored submission.

## Redirect after submitting

By default, the visitor sees a JSON response after submitting. To send them to a thank-you page, set a **redirect URL** in the form's settings in the FormWire dashboard. You don't add anything to your markup. See [Managing Forms](/guides/managing-forms).

## Spam protection

Add a field named `_fw_hp` and hide it with CSS. People never see it, and bots that fill in every field give themselves away:

```html
<input
  type="text"
  name="_fw_hp"
  tabindex="-1"
  autocomplete="off"
  style="position: absolute; left: -9999px"
  aria-hidden="true"
/>
```

You can also add a timing field that lets FormWire flag forms submitted within 2 seconds of loading. [Spam Protection](/guides/spam-protection) covers both fields.

## Field names

- Choose `name` attributes you won't need to change, because they become the keys in your stored data.
- FormWire reserves the names `_fw_hp` and `_fw_loaded` and removes those fields before storing a submission.
- A submission can be at most **50&nbsp;KB** and must contain at least one field.
