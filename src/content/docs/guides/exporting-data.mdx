Your submissions are your data. This page covers how to get them out of FormWire.

## Built-in export

:::note[Coming soon]
One-click **CSV** and **JSON** export from the dashboard is on the roadmap. This page will be updated with the details when it ships.
:::

## Getting your data today

Until built-in export lands, you have a couple of options:

- **Forward submissions with a webhook.** Connect a [webhook integration](/integrations/webhooks) to a destination you control (a spreadsheet service, a database endpoint, a serverless function). New submissions are delivered as JSON in real time, giving you a continuous copy of your data.
- **Request an export.** If you need a bulk export of existing submissions, contact support and we'll help.

## A note on data ownership

You own the submissions you collect through FormWire — see our [Terms of Service](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy). When you delete a form or your account, the associated data is removed, so export anything you need to keep beforehand.
