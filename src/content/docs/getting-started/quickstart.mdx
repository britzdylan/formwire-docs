Get your first form submission flowing in under 5 minutes.

<Steps>

1. ### Create an account

   <LinkButton
     href="https://portal.formwire.app/auth/signup"
     variant="primary"
     icon="external"
     iconPlacement="start"
   >
     Sign up for a free FormWire account
   </LinkButton>

2. ### Create a form

   From your dashboard, click **New Form** and give it a name. FormWire generates a unique **form secret** and a submission endpoint for you.

   Your endpoint will look like:

   ```txt
   https://api.formwire.app/v1/submit/YOUR_FORM_SECRET
   ```

   You can copy it straight from the form's settings.

3. ### Add to your frontend

   Point any HTML form at your FormWire endpoint:

   ```html
   <form action="https://api.formwire.app/v1/submit/YOUR_FORM_SECRET" method="POST">
     <input type="text" name="name" placeholder="Your name" required />
     <input type="email" name="email" placeholder="Your email" required />
     <textarea name="message" placeholder="Your message"></textarea>
     <button type="submit">Send</button>
   </form>
   ```

   Each `name` attribute becomes a field in your submission data.

4. ### Submit and verify

   Open your form in a browser, fill it out, and submit. Head back to your FormWire dashboard you should see the submission appear, along with the captured metadata.

</Steps>

:::tip[Want a thank-you page?]
By default, a plain HTML form post gets a JSON response. To send visitors to a thank-you page instead, set a **redirect URL** on the form (see [Managing Forms](/guides/managing-forms)).
:::
